Two lines to my past self for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty: one warning about a single moment, one sentence on why it mattered. Don't click deploy at 4:47 p.m. on the Friday before launch — that one click takes the checkout page down for nine hours, and the apology email you write that night is still the first thing anyone finds when they search your name.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (229 characters, exactly two lines, no trailing newline).
